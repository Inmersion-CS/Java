# Code of Conduct

Inmersion-CS projects are learning spaces as well as software projects. Communicate with clarity, patience, and respect. Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people; give specific feedback; respect differences in experience, language, background, identity, and viewpoint; and help beginners ask questions without ridicule.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable. Maintainers may moderate contributions or participation when conduct materially harms the project or its contributors.
